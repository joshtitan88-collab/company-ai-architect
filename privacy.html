Privacy

Privacy by architecture, not a badge farm.

Company AI Architect is a private-AI audit and implementation practice run by Joshua Henry. This page is the honest version of how data moves.

This website

The public site has no visitor account and no advertising or analytics pixel. Fonts and Sam's fallback media are hosted with the site. Your browser may keep a small local marker so Sam can recognize a returning visit; you can remove it by clearing this site's browser data.

Talking with Sam

Text you send to Sam is processed by our server-side AI provider so she can answer. When the real-time face is enabled, Tavus and Daily process the live avatar session, including the media needed to render and deliver it. Do not share customer records, medical or legal information, passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive data with Sam. We send the sales team a short structured summary when you book, not the full conversation transcript.

Booking

If you book, we collect the name, work email, company, optional phone number, requested time, and business need you provide. The booking is recorded in our private workflow, and—when calendar integration is enabled—Google Calendar receives the details needed to create the event and send an invitation. Confirmation email may be delivered by our email provider.

Optional deposits

Deposits are optional and the discovery call remains free. Stripe hosts the checkout and handles payment-card information directly. Card numbers and security codes never pass through or get stored by this website.

Discovery and paid work

Email and booking details you send us are used to arrange the call and scope work. The call is a fit check. Paid audits and implementations happen on hardware you control, or on our tower only for the duration of the engagement, then the package is yours.

The product

When privacy is required, models and knowledge stay on a tower, mini, or machine at your site. We do not sell metered access to your customers. We do not train a public model on your files. You keep the audit report and the private bundle even if you do not continue.
