Privacy

Privacy by architecture, not a badge farm.

Company AI Architect is a private-AI audit and install practice run by Joshua Henry. This page is the honest version of how data moves.

This website

The public site is static files plus a booking endpoint. There is no account, no cookie banner, and no analytics pixel. Fonts are hosted with the site. If you book a discovery, the request is sent to Joshua so he can confirm by email. This page does not keep a customer database. If you never send a request, we never see it.

Discovery and paid work

Email you send us is read by Joshua to book a call and scope work. Do not put customer names, job notes, medical, legal, or payment data in that first message. The call is a fit check. Paid audits and installs happen on hardware you control, or on our tower only for the duration of the engagement, then the package is yours.

The product

When privacy is required, models and knowledge stay on a tower, mini, or machine at your site. We do not sell metered access to your customers. We do not train a public model on your files. You keep the audit report and the private bundle even if you do not continue.
