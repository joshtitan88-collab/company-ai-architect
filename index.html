Process

From messy reality to a private AI pilot

Five stages. One engagement. You keep the report either way.

01

Describe the business

Company, industry, tools, pain points, goals. Upload SOPs or notes if you have them. We model departments and processes — not generic “use ChatGPT” advice.

02

Rank opportunities by ROI

Each opportunity scores impact, effort, build type (buy / integrate / local RAG / custom local), and estimated hours saved per week.

03

Ship the package

Full audit PDF, sales one-pager, recommended stack, 90-day plan, and a private local AI bundle with an ask.sh entrypoint when custom local wins.
